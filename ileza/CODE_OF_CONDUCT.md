# Code of conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully and constructively. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Assume good intent, keep technical criticism specific, and give people room to correct mistakes. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact published on [bahdanhal.pl](https://bahdanhal.pl/). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
